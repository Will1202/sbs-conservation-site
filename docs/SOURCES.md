# Sources and Project Materials

This page records the role of the sources and materials used in the conservation education website. It separates published ecological context from the course project’s illustrative case materials.

## Published Sources

| Source | Role in the Site |
| --- | --- |
| [IUCN Red List: Spoon-billed Sandpiper](https://www.iucnredlist.org/species/21474/45174127) | Species assessment and Critically Endangered classification. |
| [Studds et al. (2017), Nature Communications, 8, 14895](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms14895) | Research on migratory shorebird declines and reliance on Yellow Sea stopovers; historical context for regional tidal-flat loss. DOI: `10.1038/ncomms14895`. |
| [EAAFP MOP12 Decision 14 document (2025)](https://eaaflyway.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/DD.14_ISSAP_for_the_Conservation_of_the_SBS.pdf) | Four-page meeting document about the species action plan, including conservation status and range context. |
| [BirdLife DataZone species factsheet](https://datazone.birdlife.org/species/factsheet/spoon-billed-sandpiper-calidris-pygmaea) | Species overview and a starting point for further reading. |

The **>65%** statistic describes historical Yellow Sea tidal-flat loss reported in the 2017 paper. It is not a current monitoring value, a population estimate, or a calculation from the supplementary spreadsheet.

The interactive habitat diagram is a simplified ecological schematic. Jiangsu is within the Yellow Sea stopover region. The diagram does not encode coordinates, distances, or an individual tracking record.

## Tongzhou Bay Case

The course project includes planning and coastal-development images. The website uses them to introduce questions about habitat fragmentation, access, commuting distance, and disturbance.

These images support an educational discussion of possible impact pathways. They do not establish a measured ecological effect or a causal finding about a particular development. Site-specific conclusions require monitoring and assessment.
